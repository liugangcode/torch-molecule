SANDMolecularEncoder¶

Pairwise similarity scores; dense or compressed molecular descriptors.

from torch_molecule import SANDMolecularEncoder

Methods

Constructor¶

class SANDMolecularEncoder(*, num_layer=7, hidden_size=1024, emb_dim=512, encoder_type='gine', num_atom_features=62, num_edge_features=16, normalize_shared_emb=True, quantize_emb=True, num_ivf_quantizers=1, num_pq_quantizers=32, ivf_codebook_size=256, ivf_threshold_ema_dead_code=0.5, ivf_ema_decay=0.8, similarity_loss_weight=1.0, regularization_strength=1.0, uniformity_loss_weight=0.5, uniformity_margin=0.2, uniformity_num_matches=1, ivf_loss_weight=0.5, pq_loss_weight=0.25, batch_size=128, epochs=100, learning_rate=0.0005, min_lr=1e-05, weight_decay=0.05, warmup_iters=10000, decay_iters=1000000, grad_clip_value=None, emb_key=None, device=None, model_name='SANDMolecularEncoder', verbose='none')[source]¶

SAND: Shape-Aware Neural Descriptor.

Parameters:

  • num_layer (int, default=7) – Number of message-passing layers.

  • hidden_size (int, default=1024) – GNN hidden width.

  • emb_dim (int, default=512) – Molecular embedding dimension.

  • encoder_type (str, default="gine") – GNN architecture: gine, gatv2, or gps.

  • num_atom_features (int, default=62) – Atom feature vocabulary size; at least 62 for the fixed RDKit features.

  • num_edge_features (int, default=16) – Edge feature vocabulary size; at least 16 for the fixed RDKit features.

  • normalize_shared_emb (bool, default=True) – Normalize backbone embeddings during training and quantized reconstructions.

  • quantize_emb (bool, default=True) – Enable IVF/PQ compression and both quantization losses. False selects dense training.

  • num_ivf_quantizers (int, default=1) – Number of IVF quantizer heads.

  • num_pq_quantizers (int, default=32) – Number of product-quantization subspaces.

  • ivf_codebook_size (int, default=256) – Number of IVF centroids per head.

  • ivf_threshold_ema_dead_code (float, default=0.5) – EMA count threshold for replacing unused IVF codes.

  • ivf_ema_decay (float, default=0.8) – IVF centroid exponential-moving-average decay.

  • similarity_loss_weight (float, default=1.0) – Weight of the soft-Spearman similarity loss.

  • regularization_strength (float, default=1.0) – Temperature of the differentiable rank function.

  • uniformity_loss_weight (float, default=0.5) – Weight of the embedding uniformity loss.

  • uniformity_margin (float, default=0.2) – Cosine-similarity margin for the uniformity penalty.

  • uniformity_num_matches (int, default=1) – Number of matches per query; must be 1.

  • ivf_loss_weight (float, default=0.5) – Weight of the IVF cosine-alignment loss.

  • pq_loss_weight (float, default=0.25) – Weight of the PQ squared-distance loss.

  • batch_size (int, default=128) – Target pairs per training batch (at least 2), or molecules per encoding batch.

  • epochs (int, default=100) – Number of training epochs.

  • learning_rate (float, default=0.0005) – Peak learning rate.

  • min_lr (float, default=0.00001) – Minimum rate after cosine decay.

  • warmup_iters (int, default=10000) – Number of optimizer steps for learning-rate warmup.

  • decay_iters (int, default=1000000) – Optimizer-step horizon for warmup and cosine decay.

  • weight_decay (float, default=0.05) – AdamW weight decay.

  • grad_clip_value (float, optional) – Maximum gradient norm. None disables clipping.

  • emb_key (str, optional) – Embedding to return: emb or emb_quantized. None selects automatically.

  • device (torch.device or str, optional) – CPU or CUDA device; defaults to CUDA when available.

  • model_name (str, default="SANDMolecularEncoder") – Estimator name.

  • verbose (str, default="none") – One of none, progress_bar, or print_statement.

References

Paper: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/winter26a.html Code: https://github.com/pfizer-opensource/SAND
